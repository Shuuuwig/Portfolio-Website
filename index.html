Turn Based RPG

Unity / C#

Turn System Architecture

I designed a modular State Machine to handle turn flow and unit actions. The system calculates priority using the formula TV = speed / round length.

  • + Units with a lower Turn Value act sooner in the initiative order
  • + Round length manipulation allows for unique boss mechanics and debuffs
  • + TV is dynamically modified by speed changes and turn advances

Combat & Targeting

Resource Based Parrying

Specific units can perform active parries during enemy turns to negate incoming damage by spending resources.

Single Multi Targeting

In addition to standard AOE and Burst, I implemented a custom system that allows players to freely select and target multiple individual units.

Procedural Roguelike Map

The map utilizes Perlin Noise to generate random seeds for room connections and layouts.

  • + Room to room navigation follows logical connection arrows with no backtracking
  • + Random event rooms provide a break from combat to test player luck
  • + Events can impact the party through health loss or beneficial buffs

Team Formation Strategy

Strategy begins before combat with a 4 party team selection and formation system.

  • + Front, Middle, and Back ranks offer specialized property modifiers
  • + Positioning grants bonuses such as increased Attack or Melee Immunity

Puzzle Platformer

Unity / C#

Dual Boomerang Interaction

The core focus is a throwing mechanic featuring two boomerangs with distinct straight and curved trajectories.

  • + Boomerangs are used to control and alter the environment to progress
  • + Various Destructible Points serve as the main interactable triggers in each level

Physics Based Environments

This project relies heavily on the Unity physics engine to drive level interactivity and challenge.

  • + Moving and falling platforms can be utilized to build paths or act as hazards
  • + Progression often requires building solutions through environmental physics manipulation
